*** Intelligence Driven Prevention ***

> In this section, I used logs generated by the SIEM to investigate domains that had been identified as malicious by the DNS sinkhole. I began my investigation using the following domain:
agrosaoxe[.]info
Within the Elastic instance, I reviewed events stored in the filebeat-* index. For the first question, I had to determine how many DNS queries had been made to agrosaoxe[.]info.
I used the following query:
dns.question.name: "agrosaoxe.info"
This query led me to identify a total of:
11 DNS queries

> I then identified the IPv4 addresses that agrosaoxe[.]info resolved to before the sinkhole configuration was deployed:
